Visitor FAQ

Common Questions

What should I wear when visiting Church 244?

Come as you are! There is no dress code at Church 244. Whether you're most comfortable in jeans and a t-shirt or Sunday casual, you'll fit right in.

Where is Church 244 located and where do I park?

We are located at 129 Center St, Suite F in Richland, MS 39218 — right off Highway 49 South, just minutes from Florence, Pearl, Brandon, and South Jackson. Free parking is directly in front of the entrance.

What do you offer for children and teenagers?

Children's Ministry is led by London & Andrew Perkins in a safe, loving environment where kids learn the Bible on their level. Teens ages 13–19 also gather every Thursday at 6:30 PM for Church 244 Youth with Nick & Shanna Weeks.

What is worship and preaching like?

Our services last about 90 minutes and feature Spirit-led contemporary praise and worship, prayer for anyone in need, and straightforward biblical preaching centered on Jesus Christ.

Do I have to RSVP before visiting?

Never — our doors are open every week! Filling out the optional form below simply lets our team greet you by name and help get your kids checked in smoothly.
